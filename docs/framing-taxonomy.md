# UGC Framing Taxonomy (user-provided, 2026-07-31)

Source: user-supplied shot vocabulary for screenplay variety. Each frame names
the CREATOR MOVEMENT and the CAMERA position/movement. This is the raw
vocabulary the timeline planner should draw from (and vary across beats),
and the motion-prompt writer must render the camera column faithfully.

| Frame name | Creator movement | Camera positioning + movement |
|---|---|---|
| Front Facing Pano | Moving, talking at the center | Still, above head level, slightly zoomed out |
| Hand Held Moving | Creator is moving, holding camera in hand | Handheld, above head level, from the side |
| Floor Shot | Creator is standing/sitting | Camera still, on the floor, shooting at floor level or angled ~45° up |
| High Angle Drop | Creator is sitting or standing | Handheld above head level on one side, then moves down to head level directly in front of the creator |
| Pan Right | Sitting/standing, facing camera | Camera pans left → right at eye level |
| Pan Left | Sitting/standing, facing camera | Camera pans right → left at eye level |
| Low Angle Table Shot | Creator sitting on chair/sofa or standing | Camera still on a table; front shot or slightly angled |
| POV Bottom Angled | Creator is walking | Camera at eye level facing downward; creator's body visible |
| Action Shot at Eye Level | Walking, turning head to look at camera | Camera follows the creator from behind while walking; creator turns and looks into the camera |
| Top High Angle | Creator is standing/sitting | Camera well above head level looking down; full body visible |
| Side Way Moving | Creator walking and talking | Camera directly from the left or right side, moving with the creator at shoulder/head level |
| Closeup Mid Low | Creator facing the camera | Camera still at chest level, from the front or the sides |
| Found It | Creator walks into frame from one side, finds the camera, looks into it | Camera still at chest or shoulder level |
| Top Angle | Creator sitting looking straight up, or lying on the floor looking at camera | Camera still, 90° top-down |
| Wide Low Angle Shot | Creator is sitting | Camera still, facing creator from the right or left side, at waist level |
| Vlog Shot | Creator walking around | Handheld at shoulder level, facing the creator from the front |

## Usage rules (from the same feedback round)

- Shots must be FAST and on point: max ~2-3s per shot, 5s only in rare, earned
  cases. Never a frame "stuck" and slowly zooming.
- Product shots / product-to-camera (p2c): motion must be decisive (fast
  punch-in, hand plants product, snap to label) — not a slow creeping push-in.
- Vary framing across consecutive beats; do not repeat the same frame type
  back-to-back; a 6s talking stretch should be split into 2-3 frames (e.g.
  talking head → p2c → back).
